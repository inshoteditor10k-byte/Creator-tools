Refund and Cancellation Policy

We value transparent transactions and customer trust. Please read our policy below regarding digital passes:

  • 10 Hours Single Player Wallet: If a technical fault prevents wallet allocation after verified payment, a manual re-credit or full refund is issued upon submission of the Razorpay transaction ID.
  • Monthly Passes: Cancellation of monthly recurring access can be requested before the next billing cycle. Fees for previously consumed access cycles are non-refundable.
  • Failed Transactions: In cases where an account was debited but Razorpay indicates payment failure or drop-off, the payment infrastructure automatically reverses the transaction back to the original source within standard banking timelines (usually 5–7 business days).
